NTT DATA PAYMENT SERVICES

During my time as an Executive, Quality Assurance at NTT DATA Payment Services, I was responsible for performing Functional and Regression Testing on POS terminals to verify their functionality and configurations. I developed and followed a structured test case and checklist to systematically test each terminal, covering different terminal functions and ensuring consistent test coverage throughout the project.

As the project progressed, I tested a total of 649 POS terminals while keeping track of their testing status and related information through a database that I created and maintained. This allowed me to organize the testing process and ensure that each terminal was properly tested according to the established requirements, including checking terminal versions and configurations when necessary.

Throughout the project, I maintained accuracy and consistency while handling the testing of all 649 POS terminals. I also participated in client callouts, contacting customers through phone calls and text messages regarding their POS terminals. This experience strengthened my ability to work systematically, pay attention to detail, and communicate effectively while carrying out QA responsibilities in a project-based environment.
